# **GAP ANALYSIS**

A process of evaluating the differences between an organization's current performance and its desired performance. Where can improvements be made in the business?

1) Define the scope of the analysis. Identifying specific area's and the desired outcomes. 
2) Gather data on the current state of the organizations. Found through surveys. 
3) Analyze the data to identify the gaps 
4) Develop a plan to bridge the gap. Goals and timeline to achieve goals! 

2 Types of Gap analysis:

1) **Technical Gap Analysis**: Evaluate current technical infrastructure and identifying any areas where it falls short of the technical capabilities required to fully utilize their security solutions.
	- Maybe its not fast or not zero trust infrastructure now that gap exists, they can plane to fix it.
2) **Business Gap Analysis**: Evaluating an organization's current business processes and identifying any areas where they fall short of the capabilities required  to fully utilize cloud based solutions.
	- Maybe current data isn't or budget enough to have cloud based services.



CREATE A POA, PLAN OF ACTION TO STEP BY STEP BRIDGE PRIORITY GAPS
